PHOTOSYNTHESIS: light-dependent rxns

1st stage happens in thylakoid membrane.
Pigment = chlorophyll a & b, absorbs ~430nm + ~662nm (blue + red). Reflects green -> that's why leaves look green.

Photon hits PSII -> electron excited -> passed down ETC -> PSI -> NADP+ + H -> NADPH
H2O is split (photolysis) to replace the lost e-.  2H2O -> 4H+ + 4e- + O2
** the O2 we breathe is a waste product. Prof said this is on the exam.

H+ builds up inside thylakoid -> gradient -> ATP synthase -> ATP  (chemiosmosis)

Outputs of stage 1: ATP, NADPH, O2
